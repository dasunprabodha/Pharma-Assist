Prescription Reader

For Pharmacist A · Dulnitha Pharmacy, Kottawa

About 200 prescriptions a day, 70–80% handwritten, each taking 2–3 minutes to decipher and match to stock — this tool uses Amazon Nova on Amazon Bedrock to read them into an editable list.

Decision support only – the pharmacist must confirm every item.
