For the best reading

  • Lay the prescription flat in good light
  • Fill the frame; avoid shadows and glare
  • One prescription per photo
  • PDF scans work too – upload only the prescription page(s), up to 3

Privacy

The photo (or each PDF page, rendered as an image) is resized and all metadata is removed before it is sent to Amazon Bedrock. Patient name, address and doctor details are ignored.
